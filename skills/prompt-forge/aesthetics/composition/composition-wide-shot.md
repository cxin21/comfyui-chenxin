# 广角远景 (Wide Shot)

## 自然语言 (Flux/Anima/Qwen/SD3.5 用)
wide establishing shot showing the full environment, subject appears small within a vast landscape, environmental storytelling through scale contrast, negative space above emphasizing scale

## Danbooru Tag (Pony/Illustrious/SDXL 用)
wide_shot, full_body, landscape, panorama

## 适用场景
古风山水、科幻远景、史诗场景
