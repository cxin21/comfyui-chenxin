# 横向全景 (Landscape)

## 自然语言 (Flux/Anima/Qwen/SD3.5 用)
landscape orientation emphasizing horizontal expanse, wide aspect ratio, subject placed off-center following rule of thirds, environment dominates the frame, deep depth of field with foreground to background sharpness

## Danbooru Tag (Pony/Illustrious/SDXL 用)
landscape, scenery, wide_shot, outdoors, depth_of_field, panorama

## 适用场景
自然风光、城市天际线、环境叙事
