# 低角度仰拍 (Low Angle)

## 自然语言 (Flux/Anima/Qwen/SD3.5 用)
low angle shot looking upward, camera positioned below eye level, subject towers over the viewer, dramatic upward perspective making the figure appear powerful and imposing, sky or ceiling visible above

## Danbooru Tag (Pony/Illustrious/SDXL 用)
from_below, looking_up, wide_shot

## 适用场景
战斗、英雄登场、反派压迫感、宏伟建筑
