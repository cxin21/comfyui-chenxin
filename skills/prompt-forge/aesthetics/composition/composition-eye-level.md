# 平视镜头 (Eye Level)

## 自然语言 (Flux/Anima/Qwen/SD3.5 用)
eye level shot at subject's height, natural perspective as if viewer is standing face to face with the subject, straightforward and unpretentious framing, documentary style neutrality
