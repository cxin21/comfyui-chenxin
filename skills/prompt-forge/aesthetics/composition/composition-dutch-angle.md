# 倾斜构图 (Dutch Angle)

## 自然语言 (Flux/Anima/Qwen/SD3.5 用)
dutch angle with camera tilted off horizontal axis by 15-30 degrees, deliberate disorientation creates unease and dynamic tension, skewed horizon line, psychological instability conveyed through framing

## Danbooru Tag (Pony/Illustrious/SDXL 用)

## 适用场景
