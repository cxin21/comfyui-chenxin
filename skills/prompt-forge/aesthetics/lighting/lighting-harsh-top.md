# 顶光强照

## 自然语言 (Flux/Anima/Qwen/SD3.5 用)
Harsh overhead industrial fluorescent light casting deep shadows downward from eye sockets and nose, high contrast with crushed blacks in the shadow areas, sterile institutional atmosphere of a laboratory or interrogation room, unflattering direct top-down illumination that carves the face into sharp planes of light and dark, greenish color cast from fluorescent tubes, cold unforgiving light quality, deep eye socket shadows creating a hollow gaunt look, flickering tube light adding unease, CCTV or security camera lighting quality
