# 自然柔光

## 自然语言 (Flux/Anima/Qwen/SD3.5 用)
Soft diffused natural light on an overcast day, gentle even shadows with no harsh edges, smooth exposure across the entire frame, no harsh highlights or blown-out areas, large soft window light quality wrapping around the subject, ambient skylight filling shadows with cool tones, wraparound illumination from a broad diffused source, very low contrast ratio around 2:1, peaceful and calm lighting mood

## Danbooru Tag (Pony/Illustrious/SDXL 用)
overcast, window, morning, outdoors

## 适用场景
日常、散步、咖啡馆、自然聊天场景
