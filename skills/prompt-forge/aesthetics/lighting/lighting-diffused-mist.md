# 迷雾漫射光

## 自然语言 (Flux/Anima/Qwen/SD3.5 用)
Soft diffused light filtered through thick layers of mountain mist and fog, ethereal glow with no clearly defined light source direction, atmospheric haze softening all edges and reducing contrast, visible light beams cutting through fog banks like celestial rays, dreamlike illumination with a pearl-like quality, distant elements fading into graduated atmospheric perspective, moisture particles catching and scattering light, cool blue-white tone with subtle warm undertones where light penetrates the mist, traditional Chinese landscape painting aesthetic
