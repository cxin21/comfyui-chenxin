# 窗边柔光

## 自然语言 (Flux/Anima/Qwen/SD3.5 用)
Soft window light entering from one side through a large window, gentle falloff across the room with brightness decreasing gradually with distance from the window, natural indoor ambient illumination with indirect bounce light filling the shadow side from walls and ceiling, warm morning or late afternoon sunlight filtered through sheer curtains creating a soft directional quality, creamy shadows with subtle detail rather than pure black, Vermeer painting quality of interior light, dust motes floating in the shaft of window light, cozy domestic atmosphere

## Danbooru Tag (Pony/Illustrious/SDXL 用)
window, sunlight, indoors, sidelighting, curtains, morning

## 适用场景
室内、日常居家、咖啡馆、安静的读书场景
