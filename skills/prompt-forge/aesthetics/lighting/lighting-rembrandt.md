# 伦勃朗光

## 自然语言 (Flux/Anima/Qwen/SD3.5 用)
Single light source positioned high and to one side forming the characteristic triangular patch of light on the shadowed cheek below the eye, dark background fading gradually to complete black, classical portrait lighting with a dramatic 4:1 to 8:1 shadow-to-light ratio, warm tungsten key light around 3200K creating depth in skin tones, the illuminated side of the face fully lit while the far side falls into deep shadow with just the signature triangle of light remaining, painterly quality reminiscent of Rembrandt's portraits, subtle catchlight in the near eye only
