# 阴天散射光

## 自然语言 (Flux/Anima/Qwen/SD3.5 用)
Flat grey overcast sky producing soft almost shadowless light from the entire sky dome acting as a giant diffuser, cool color temperature around 6500K-7500K giving a blue-grey cast to everything, muted desaturated tones with reduced color vibrancy, rain or light snow falling through the frame adding atmosphere, wet pavement and surfaces reflecting the grey sky above, melancholic subdued atmosphere with no bright highlights anywhere in the scene, visibility reduced by precipitation and atmospheric moisture, the kind of light where shadows simply do not exist, everything evenly and flatly illuminated from above
