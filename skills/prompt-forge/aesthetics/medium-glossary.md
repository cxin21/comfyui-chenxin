# Medium glossary

Describe a medium through visible material behavior. Prefer concrete properties over prestige labels.

| Medium | Useful visible language |
|---|---|
| ink wash | pooled ink, feathered edges, dry-brush accents, paper fibers, deliberate open areas |
| oil painting | layered brushwork, opaque pigment, canvas tooth, soft edge transitions |
| watercolor | transparent washes, pigment blooms, granulating color, soft paper texture |
| charcoal drawing | compressed charcoal, rubbed shadows, broken contour, rough paper grain |
| cinematic photography | lens depth, controlled highlight rolloff, natural material response, subtle grain |
| documentary photography | available light, observational framing, unpolished surface detail |
| flat illustration | clean silhouette, deliberate shape hierarchy, limited palette, crisp edges |
| pixel art | visible pixel grid, clustered shading, limited palette, stepped contours |
| paper cutout | layered paper planes, cut edges, small cast shadows, tactile fibers |
| embroidery | thread direction, stitch density, fabric weave, raised highlights |

## Combination guidance

Combine media only when the requested art direction describes a hybrid. Name how the materials meet, such as watercolor washes beneath graphite contour. Otherwise keep one coherent medium and describe its surface, edge, color, and depth behavior.

Medium language never changes who or what appears in the scene.
