# Video prompt dialects

Video prompting directs visible change over time while preserving the evidence contract.

## Bilingual shot description

Provide `positive_zh`, `positive_en`, and a concise `global_prompt`. Chinese and English describe the same subject, action, setting, and endpoint. Neither language may add a fact missing from the other.

## Timeline language

- Begin at the established opening state.
- Use contiguous ranges and one clear beat per segment.
- Name the moving subject, path or body action, speed when relevant, and endpoint.
- Attribute every spoken line to a speaker and place it within a valid range.
- Repeat persistent conditions in continuity locks: identity cues, wardrobe, props, spatial relationships, weather, and direction of travel.

## Style variants

A video style variant may change medium, palette, lighting, texture, camera feel, and motion quality. It cannot change the event order, action result, dialogue, prop ownership, identity, or continuity locks.

## Uncertainty

If timing, speaker, endpoint, or reference role is unclear, record the uncertainty. Do not fill temporal gaps with a plausible new beat.
